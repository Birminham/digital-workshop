# ProductHunt Launch Checklist

- [ ] Deploy all 3 tools to live URLs (Vercel/Cloudflare Pages)
- [ ] Deploy Product Hub as main landing page
- [ ] Set up Google Analytics on all pages
- [ ] Create ProductHunt maker profile
- [ ] Prepare screenshots + GIFs (5 per tool)

## Launch Day (pick 1 tool at a time, 1 week apart)

### Tool 1: Screenshot Beautifier (launch first — most visual)

**PH Tagline:** "Turn raw screenshots into stunning marketing assets — no design skills"

**First comment (paste immediately after listing):**
```
Hey ProductHunt! 👋

I built Screenshot Beautifier because I was tired of opening Figma just to frame a screenshot for Twitter.

What it does:
• Drag in any screenshot
• Wrap it in an iPhone/Mac/Chrome frame
• Add gradient backgrounds, shadows, watermarks
• Export at 3× resolution
• Everything runs in your browser — zero uploads

It's 100% free with no signup. The entire tool is ~100KB and works offline after first load.

Why I built it:
As an indie hacker, I spend a lot of time making screenshots look good for landing pages and social media. This tool does in 30 seconds what used to take me 5 minutes in a design tool.

Would love your feedback! What frames or backgrounds should I add next?
```

### Tool 2: Image Crush (launch week 2)

**PH Tagline:** "Batch compress images in your browser — zero uploads, instant preview"

**First comment:**
```
Hey makers! 🗜️

Image Crush is a batch image compressor that never uploads your files anywhere.

Why this exists:
Every "free image compressor" site either:
• Limits how many images you can compress
• Uploads your files to their server (privacy risk)
• Adds watermarks
• Tries to sell you a "pro" plan

Image Crush does none of that. It's genuinely free and local:
• Batch drop unlimited images
• Convert between JPEG/PNG/WebP/AVIF
• Drag-to-compare before/after slider
• ZIP download everything
• All Canvas API — nothing leaves your browser

Tested real-world: 3 PNGs (1.44 MB) → JPEG 80% (232 KB). 84% smaller, zero visible quality loss.

What format should I add next? HEIC? TIFF?
```

### Tool 3: PDF Magic (launch week 3)

**PH Tagline:** "The PDF toolkit that never sees your files — merge, split, compress, convert"

**First comment:**
```
Hi ProductHunt 📄

PDF Magic is a full PDF toolkit that runs 100% in your browser via WebAssembly.

I built this because uploading sensitive documents to random PDF sites always felt wrong. Tax returns, contracts, medical records — they shouldn't leave your computer just to merge two pages.

What it does:
• Merge PDFs (drag to reorder)
• Split by page range
• Compress image-heavy PDFs
• Convert to/from images
• Password-protect files
• All offline after first load

The entire toolkit is free. No watermark. No "3 free merges then pay." No account needed.

What PDF feature do you wish existed?
```
