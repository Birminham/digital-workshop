# Screenshot Beautifier ✨

**Turn raw screenshots into stunning marketing assets in seconds.**

---

## One-Liner

Drag, drop, frame, export. Turn any screenshot into a beautiful social-ready image — no design skills needed.

---

## Product Description

Screenshot Beautifier is the fastest way to make your screenshots look professional. Whether you're shipping a feature announcement, writing a blog post, or sharing a customer review — raw screenshots look boring. Beautifier wraps them in device frames, adds gradient backgrounds, shadows, and watermarks in one click.

No signup. No upload to any server. Everything runs in your browser.

---

## Key Features

### 🖼️ 5 Device Frames
- **iPhone 15 Pro** — Realistic bezel with Dynamic Island, perfect for mobile screenshots
- **Mac Safari** — macOS window chrome with traffic lights and address bar
- **Chrome Browser** — Clean browser frame with omnibox
- **Shadow Card** — Floating card with adjustable shadow depth
- **No Frame** — Just the image with effects

### 🎨 7 Gradient Backgrounds + Custom
- Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Neon Purple, Warm Gold, Pure White, Pure Black
- Custom two-color gradient picker

### 🎯 Pro Effects
- **Padding** — 0–100px breathing room
- **Shadow** — Intensity slider from subtle to dramatic
- **Rounded Corners** — 0–48px radius
- **Watermark** — Custom text, 3 positions, adjustable opacity

### 📤 Export Options
- PNG export at 1×, 2×, or 3× resolution
- Copy to clipboard (one click)
- Auto-filename with timestamp

## How It Works

```
1. Drag & drop your screenshot
2. Pick a device frame
3. Choose a background gradient
4. Adjust padding, shadow, corners
5. Add watermark (optional)
6. Export PNG — done.
```

Entire workflow: < 30 seconds.
